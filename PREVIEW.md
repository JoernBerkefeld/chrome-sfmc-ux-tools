# Preview features

Preview features are features of SFMC UX Tools that are built but not finished. They are switched off after installing and the options do not show them.

- **There are no preview features at the moment.**

## How to see the preview features

- Click the extension's icon in Chrome's toolbar to open the options.
- Hold **Alt** and click the **©** sign at the bottom right.
- A group **Preview features** appears. At the moment it only says that there are none.

## When there is one again

- Choose **on** for the feature in that group.
- The change applies at once; no reload is needed.
- While a preview feature is on, the group shows by itself, so that you can switch it off again.
